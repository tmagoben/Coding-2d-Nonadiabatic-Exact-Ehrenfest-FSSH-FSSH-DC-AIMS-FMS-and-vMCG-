# v0.25.2 adaptive multi-Gaussian TDVP: mathematical specification

## 1. Hamiltonian and state space

The released Hamiltonian remains

$$
\hat H=-\frac{1}{2m}\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\mathbf 1_{N_s}
+H_0+xH_1+x^2H_2,
\qquad m>0,\qquad H_k=H_k^\dagger,
$$

in one coordinate-independent complete electronic frame. The quadratic-model intake
is inherited from v0.25.1 and independently audits values, physical derivatives,
zero connection, state vectors, and complete-multiplet provenance.

## 2. Adaptive normalized packet

For packet `I`, write `y_I=x-q_I` and

$$
g_I(x)=\left(\frac{\alpha_I}{\pi}\right)^{1/4}
\exp\left[-\frac{\alpha_I}{2}y_I^2
+\frac{i\beta_I}{2}y_I^2+ip_Iy_I\right],
\qquad \alpha_I>0,\quad\beta_I\in\mathbb{R}.
$$

The wavefunction is

$$
|\Psi\rangle=\sum_{I=1}^{N_g}g_I(x)
\sum_{a=1}^{N_s}C_{Ia}|a\rangle.
$$

v0.25.2 propagates `eta_I=log(alpha_I)` rather than raw `alpha_I`. Its real parameter
order is

$$
\theta=(\operatorname{Re}C,\operatorname{Im}C,q,p,\eta,\beta),
\qquad P=2N_gN_s+4N_g.
$$

Exponentiation reconstructs every positive width, including at nonlinear midpoint
states. Arithmetic and configured-domain limits still fail closed.

## 3. Why width and chirp must be paired

A varying real envelope needs a quadratic phase to represent its conjugate flow.
Define

$$z_I=\alpha_I-i\beta_I.$$

Then `g_I` has exponent `-z_I y_I^2/2+ip_Iy_I`. Allowing only `alpha_I` to move
would omit the phase curvature generated by kinetic propagation. Pairing `eta_I` and
`beta_I` gives a closed normalized thawed-Gaussian manifold for a quadratic scalar
Hamiltonian.

## 4. Exact tangent vectors

The coefficient tangents remain

$$
\partial_{\operatorname{Re}C_{Ia}}\Psi=g_I|a\rangle,
\qquad
\partial_{\operatorname{Im}C_{Ia}}\Psi=i g_I|a\rangle.
$$

The packet tangents are

$$
\partial_{q_I}g_I=[z_Iy_I-ip_I]g_I,
\qquad
\partial_{p_I}g_I=iy_Ig_I,
$$

$$
\partial_{\eta_I}g_I=\left(\frac14-\frac{\alpha_I}{2}y_I^2\right)g_I,
\qquad
\partial_{\beta_I}g_I=\frac{i}{2}y_I^2g_I.
$$

The largest tangent polynomial degree is therefore two.

## 5. McLachlan metric

Minimizing

$$\|\dot\Psi+i\hat H\Psi\|^2$$

over the real velocities yields

$$
G_{\mu\nu}\dot\theta_\nu=b_\mu,
\quad
G_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{Re}
\langle\partial_\mu\Psi|\partial_\nu\Psi\rangle,
\quad
b_\mu=\operatorname{Im}
\langle\partial_\mu\Psi|\hat H\Psi\rangle.
$$

`G` is real symmetric positive semidefinite. Duplicate or otherwise redundant
packets can produce exact tangent null directions; they are handled by the inherited
compatible-null SVD policy rather than diagonal loading.

## 6. Exact chirped cross moments

For bra packet `I` and ket packet `J`, define

$$
z_I^*=\alpha_I+i\beta_I,\qquad z_J=\alpha_J-i\beta_J,
$$

$$
B=z_I^*+z_J,qquad
\ell=z_I^*q_I+z_Jq_J+i(p_J-p_I),
$$

$$
c=-\frac12z_I^*q_I^2-\frac12z_Jq_J^2
+ip_Iq_I-ip_Jq_J.
$$

Because `Re(B)=alpha_I+alpha_J>0`, the principal Gaussian integral is unique:

$$
S_{IJ}=\sqrt{2}\,(\alpha_I\alpha_J)^{1/4}B^{-1/2}
\exp\left(c+\frac{\ell^2}{2B}\right).
$$

With complex centroid `mu=ell/B` and covariance `v=1/B`,

$$
M_0=S,\quad M_1=S\mu,\quad M_2=S(\mu^2+v),
$$

$$
M_3=S(\mu^3+3\mu v),\qquad
M_4=S(\mu^4+6\mu^2v+3v^2).
$$

Metric products require degree `2+2=4`. The RHS also requires degree four because a
degree-two bra tangent multiplies the degree-two Hamiltonian action.

## 7. Kinetic polynomial

For ket packet `J`,

$$
-\frac{1}{2m}g_J''=
\left[\frac{p_J^2+z_J}{2m}
+\frac{iz_Jp_J}{m}y_J
-\frac{z_J^2}{2m}y_J^2\right]g_J.
$$

Expanding `y_J=x-q_J` turns this into a degree-two polynomial in `x`, which contracts
directly with `M_0,M_1,M_2`. Production uses no spatial grid or numerical derivative.
Independent tests reconstruct the same overlap, Hamiltonian, full metric, and RHS by
dense-grid quadrature and finite-difference tangents.

## 8. Harmonic reduction

For `V(x)=m omega^2 x^2/2`, the exact continuous packet equations are

$$
\dot q=\frac{p}{m},\qquad \dot p=-m\omega^2q,
$$

$$
\dot\eta=-\frac{2\beta}{m},\qquad
\dot\beta=\frac{\alpha^2-\beta^2}{m}-m\omega^2.
$$

Let `A=beta+i alpha`. It satisfies

$$\dot A=-\frac{A^2}{m}-m\omega^2.$$

The closed-form endpoint used by validation is

$$
A(t)=\frac{-m\omega\sin(\omega t)+A_0\cos(\omega t)}
{\cos(\omega t)+A_0\sin(\omega t)/(m\omega)}.
$$

Its real and imaginary parts give chirp and width. When `alpha=m omega` and
`beta=0`, both width velocities vanish and the adaptive trajectory reduces to the
v0.25.1 frozen coherent-state result.

## 9. SVD and implicit midpoint

The metric velocity is the compatible minimum-norm pseudoinverse solution. A signed
step solves

$$
R(\theta_{n+1})=\theta_{n+1}-\theta_n
-h\,G^+\!\left(\frac{\theta_n+\theta_{n+1}}2\right)
b\!\left(\frac{\theta_n+\theta_{n+1}}2\right)=0.
$$

Equivalently, `R=theta_1-theta_0-h*v(theta_mid)`. Every receipt reconstructs the
log-width midpoint (the geometric mean in raw width), full metric/SVD velocity,
residual, endpoint energy, norm, and width-domain checks.

## 10. Exact scope

“Adaptive width” in v0.25.2 means one positive scalar width and one scalar chirp per
one-dimensional packet. It does not mean multidimensional diagonal/full correlated
width matrices, spawning, pruning, moving electronic frames, or ab-initio molecular
SOC trajectories.
