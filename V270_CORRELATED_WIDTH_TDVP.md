# v0.27.0 Correlated-Width TDVP

## Packet convention

For packet $I$ and $D$ nuclear coordinates,

$$
g_I(R)=\left(\frac{\det\Gamma_I}{\pi^D}\right)^{1/4}
\exp\left[-\frac12 x_I^T(\Gamma_I-iB_I)x_I+i p_I^Tx_I\right],
\qquad x_I=R-q_I.
$$

$\Gamma_I$ is real symmetric positive definite and $B_I$ is real symmetric. The
width is parameterized as

$$
\Gamma_I=\exp(E_I),\qquad E_I=E_I^T,
$$

so a nonlinear iterate cannot cross the positive-definite boundary.

## Orthonormal symmetric coordinates

For a symmetric matrix $A$, `svec(A)` leaves diagonal entries unchanged and stores
$\sqrt2 A_{ab}$ for $a<b$. Therefore

$$
\operatorname{svec}(A)^T\operatorname{svec}(C)=\operatorname{Tr}(A^TC).
$$

If $R'=OR$ with $O^TO=I$, then $A'=OAO^T$. The corresponding map in `svec`
space is orthogonal. This property is essential: an isotropic numerical scaling of
the packed midpoint residual does not introduce a preferred laboratory direction.

## Cross Gaussian

For a bra packet $i$ and ket packet $j$, define

$$
A=(\Gamma_i+iB_i)+(\Gamma_j-iB_j),
$$

$$
h=(\Gamma_i+iB_i)q_i+(\Gamma_j-iB_j)q_j+i(p_j-p_i),
$$

and $\Sigma=A^{-1}$, $\mu=\Sigma h$. Completing the square gives the overlap and
turns every polynomial matrix element into a raw moment of a complex multivariate
normal distribution. Moments through total degree four are generated by

$$
M_{n+e_a}=\mu_aM_n+\sum_b n_b\Sigma_{ab}M_{n-e_b}.
$$

The overlap phase uses the sum of individual positive-stable eigenvalue logarithms,
avoiding an artificial wrap of the determinant phase.

## Kinetic action

With $K=\Gamma-iB$,

$$
\partial_a g=f_a g,\qquad f_a=-[K(R-q)]_a+ip_a,
$$

$$
\partial_a\partial_b g=(f_af_b-K_{ab})g.
$$

For an arbitrary positive-definite constant mass matrix,

$$
Tg=-\frac12\sum_{ab}(M^{-1})_{ab}(f_af_b-K_{ab})g.
$$

## Full tangent set

The real parameter vector is ordered as

$$
\theta=(\Re C,\Im C,q,p,\operatorname{svec}E,\operatorname{svec}B).
$$

For a symmetric basis direction $F_k$, the width derivative is

$$
d\Gamma=D\exp(E)[F_k],
$$

and

$$
\frac{\partial g}{\partial E_k}=
\left[\frac14\operatorname{Tr}(\Gamma^{-1}d\Gamma)
-\frac12x^Td\Gamma x\right]g.
$$

The chirp tangent is $(i/2)x^TF_kx\,g$. The matrix-exponential Frechet derivative
is evaluated exactly in the eigenbasis using the exponential divided difference.

## McLachlan equation and solver

The code minimizes

$$
\left\|\dot\Psi+iH\Psi\right\|^2
$$

over real $\dot\theta$, producing

$$
A_{\mu\nu}=\Re\langle\partial_\mu\Psi|\partial_\nu\Psi\rangle,
\qquad
b_\mu=\Im\langle\partial_\mu\Psi|H\Psi\rangle,
\qquad A\dot\theta=b.
$$

A full SVD retains only singular directions above the absolute/relative cutoff and
rejects an incompatible null-space forcing. The nonlinear time step solves

$$
r(\theta_{n+1})=\theta_{n+1}-\theta_n
-\Delta t\,v\!\left(\frac{\theta_n+\theta_{n+1}}2\right)=0.
$$

All packed coordinates receive identical nonlinear-solver scaling. Width eigenvalue,
chirp-radius, log-width-step, nonlinear residual, norm, and SVD receipts are checked
before the step is admitted.

## Exact reductions

When $D=1$, `svec` is the identity and the full matrix manifold is exactly the
v0.26.0 scalar-per-coordinate manifold. Overlap, Hamiltonian, metric, forcing,
velocity, and implicit midpoint endpoint agree numerically. In higher dimensions,
diagonal states reproduce v0.26.0 overlap, Hamiltonian, energy, and density, while
the additional off-diagonal tangents provide the intentionally larger manifold.
